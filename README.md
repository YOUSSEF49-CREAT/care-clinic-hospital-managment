# Care / Clinic / Hospital Management System

A comprehensive **Care / Clinic / Hospital Management System** designed to streamline and automate day-to-day healthcare operations such as patient management, appointment scheduling, doctor management, billing, and medical records.

This system improves efficiency, reduces paperwork, and enhances patient care through a centralized digital platform.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 👤 Patient Management
- Add, update, and delete patient records
- Maintain medical history and treatment details
- Patient search and record tracking

### 👨‍⚕️ Doctor Management
- Manage doctor profiles and specialties
- Assign doctors to patients
- View doctor availability

### 📅 Appointment Management
- Schedule, reschedule, and cancel appointments
- Avoid scheduling conflicts
- Appointment status tracking

### 🏥 Clinic / Hospital Management
- Manage departments and rooms
- Track admissions and discharges
- Staff management support

### 💳 Billing & Payments
- Generate patient bills
- Track payments and pending dues
- Invoice management

### 📊 Reports & Analytics
- Patient reports
- Appointment statistics
- Financial summaries

### 🔐 Authentication & Security
- Role-based access (Admin, Doctor, Receptionist)
- Secure login and logout
- Data privacy and access control
